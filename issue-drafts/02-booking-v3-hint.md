# [4471 split] booking-v3 date hint conflicts with day-first tenants — 33 reports

## Problem

The booking-v3 form tells people to enter dates as `MM/DD/YYYY`. In day-first tenants, that hint conflicts with how people normally write dates, producing a separate booking-form problem. The export's tenant, locale, and channel data place 33 unique reports in this cause.

## Outcome

The engineering update on the Challenge page says the booking-v3 form was retired and this problem will not be fixed. Close this ticket as not planned, not as completed.
